# 👥 User Personas

## 1. The B2B Buyer (Contractor / Procurement Manager)
**Demographics:** 30-55 years old, construction industry professional.
**Goals:** Quickly find construction chemicals, check technical specs (via the Knowledge Base), and place bulk orders seamlessly.
**Pain Points:** Dislikes calling for inventory checks; needs reliable tracking for site deliveries.
**Key Features Used:** Product Catalog, Related Products, Cart, Order Timeline, Profile (Default Address).

## 2. The Operations Admin
**Demographics:** Internal employee managing day-to-day warehouse and digital operations.
**Goals:** Keep the product catalog up to date, process orders rapidly, and publish technical blogs.
**Pain Points:** Manual data entry across hundreds of products.
**Key Features Used:** Admin Dashboard, CSV Bulk Upload, Order Management, Blog Editor.

## 3. The SuperAdmin (Business Owner / Executive)
**Demographics:** Stakeholder focused on business growth and platform health.
**Goals:** Monitor revenue, track highest-performing products, and oversee system access.
**Pain Points:** Lack of real-time visibility into sales metrics.
**Key Features Used:** SuperAdmin Dashboard (Analytics), User Management.

# 🔍 Product Detail
- **Images:** Left side gallery (main image + thumbnails).
- **Details:** Right side: Title, SKU, Status Badge (In Stock), Price, Description.
- **Purchase Action:** Quantity selector (for bulk), prominent "Add to Cart" button.
- **Tabs:** Specifications, Technical Data Sheets (Download links), Reviews.
- **Bottom:** "Related Products" horizontal scroll or grid.

# 🎨 Color Palette

## 1. Brand Colors
- **Primary:** Tailwind `blue-600` (`#2563eb`) — Used for primary actions, active states, and links.
- **Secondary:** Tailwind `slate-800` (`#1e293b`) — Used for secondary buttons and footer backgrounds.
- **Accent:** Tailwind `amber-500` (`#f59e0b`) — Used for alerts, warnings, and highlighting low stock.

## 2. UI Colors
- **Background (Light):** `bg-gray-50` for app background, `bg-white` for cards.
- **Background (Dark):** `bg-gray-900` for app background, `bg-gray-800` for cards.
- **Text (Light mode):** `text-gray-900` for headings, `text-gray-600` for body.
- **Text (Dark mode):** `text-gray-100` for headings, `text-gray-400` for body.

## 3. Semantic Colors
- **Success:** `green-500` (Order success, in-stock).
- **Danger:** `red-500` (Delete actions, out of stock, errors).
- **Warning:** `yellow-500` (Pending orders).
- **Info:** `blue-500` (Informational alerts).
